CSC 240 - Lab 1: Tic-Tac-Toe
Reference Game Transcript

This shows the board rendering, the prompt wording, and the handling of an occupied
square that your program must match. Rows and columns are numbered 1 to 3. A move is
entered as two numbers separated by a space: row first, then column.

YOUR OUTPUT WILL NOT MATCH THIS LINE FOR LINE, and it is not supposed to. play()
picks the starting player at random, so about half your runs will open with O
instead of X. What must match is the formatting and the behaviour, not the moves.

Watch the fifth prompt in particular. Player X enters "3 1", which X already
occupies. The move is rejected and X is prompted again. Play does NOT pass to O -
a rejected move costs the player nothing but a retry.

WHAT TO CHECK IN YOUR OWN OUTPUT

1. The board prints once before the first prompt, and once after every accepted
   move. It does not print after a rejected move.

2. The prompt is exactly:  Player X enter move:
   with a single space after the colon, and X or O as appropriate.

3. An occupied square produces the rejection message and re-prompts the SAME
   player. isValidMove is what decides this.

4. A win is announced as:  Player X wins!
   A full board with no winner is announced as:  The game is a draw.

5. Wins must be detected on all eight lines: three rows, three columns, and both
   diagonals. This transcript only exercises one of them - test the rest
   yourself before you submit.

6. Out-of-range input, such as "4 1" or "0 2", is also an invalid move and must
   be rejected the same way rather than crashing or writing outside the array.
